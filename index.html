Best image size for an Instagram carousel

For a vertical carousel with a 4:5 aspect ratio, a practical size is 1080 × 1350 pixels per slide. Prepare a single wide image and split it into equal sections:

  • 3 slides: 3240 × 1350 pixels, with 1 row and 3 columns.
  • 5 slides: 5400 × 1350 pixels, with 1 row and 5 columns.
  • 7 slides: 7560 × 1350 pixels, with 1 row and 7 columns.

The tool splits your original image into equal sections. It does not automatically change the aspect ratio of each slide. For the cleanest result, prepare the original canvas at the correct dimensions.

How to create a seamless carousel

Design your full carousel as one long image, placing each slide at the same width. Keep important text and faces away from the split boundaries. Then upload the full image, choose 1 row and the appropriate number of columns, and download the individual slides.

Want to make a grid or mosaic instead? You can also use multiple rows and columns. The image is divided evenly using the values you select.
